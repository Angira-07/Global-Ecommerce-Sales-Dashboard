## 💡 Key Insights

- 🔴 **Office Supplies** is the only loss-making category — shipping costs eat the profit
- 🟢 **Furniture** drives the most of revenue and highest profit
- 👤 **Consumer segment** contributes the most at 52.89% of total revenue
- 💳 **Credit Card** dominates at 39.77% of all transactions
- 🌍 **Europe + North America** together = 55%+ of total revenue
- ✅ **86.4%** of all orders are Healthy — strong overall order quality
